Our Story

Born from a card that made grandma cry.

In 2018, our founder mailed a clunky DIY video card to a grandmother in Lisbon. The screen flickered, the speaker buzzed — but she cried. That tiny, imperfect moment became the brief: build the gift that does this every single time.

Today, the Superlieur factory in Shenzhen produces over 1.2 million video greeting cards a year for boutique retailers and gift brands across North America, Europe and the Middle East.
