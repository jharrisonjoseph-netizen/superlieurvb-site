Video Gift Boxes

Solid-wood heirloom enclosures with embedded HD screens. The unboxing becomes the moment — and the box stays on the desk.

Range overview

Heirloom wood, modern electronics.

Each box is CNC-cut from FSC-certified walnut, beech or bamboo, finished with natural oil and lined with velvet. A 4.3″ or 5″ IPS screen is integrated into the lid and auto-plays when opened.

  • FSC-certified walnut, beech or bamboo wood
  • 4.3″ or 5″ IPS HD screen with magnetic auto-play switch
  • 1 GB to 8 GB storage · up to 8 hours of HD video
  • 1,200–2,000 mAh battery · USB-C charging
  • Velvet-lined interior with optional jewellery insert
  • Magnetic or hinged lid closure
