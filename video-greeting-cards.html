Video Greeting Cards

The most thoughtful card you can mail — a slim, beautifully bound greeting that opens to a full-colour HD video message.

Range overview

14 stock models. Endless OEM options.

From a pocket-sized 2.4-inch screen to a stunning 10-inch foldable, every model balances battery life, video quality and mailable form factor. All ship with a USB-C upload port, rechargeable battery and auto-play on opening.

  • 2.4″ / 4.3″ / 5″ / 7″ / 10″ IPS HD screens (1080p on 7″+)
  • Up to 8 GB storage · ≈ 8 hours of HD video
  • 500–1,500 mAh battery · 2–4 h continuous playback
  • Auto-play on opening with magnetic switch
  • USB-C upload + OTG smartphone upload
  • FCC / CE / RoHS / REACH / PSE certified
