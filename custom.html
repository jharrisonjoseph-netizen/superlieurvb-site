The process

Six steps. Predictable timeline. No surprises.

Brief & NDA

Share your concept, target price, channels and deadline. We sign NDA within 24 hours.

Concept & quote

3 industrial design directions + indicative pricing in 5 working days. Free for orders over 5k pcs.

SLA prototype

3D-printed appearance models in 2 days; functional prototype with electronics in 10 days.

Mould & tooling

If new enclosure: $1.5k–$8k tooling fee, refundable across 10k pcs. T-1 mould samples in 12 days.

Certification

We manage FCC, CE, RoHS, REACH and PSE testing at SGS or TÜV — 10–15 working days.

Mass production

18–25 working days for the first 5,000 pcs after PP approval. 100% pre-shipment QC.

What's customisable

If you can sketch it, we can build it.

Hardware

Screen size (1.8″ to 13.3″), enclosure shape & material, battery, speaker, switch type, sensors (light, motion, NFC).

Firmware

Custom boot animation, voice prompts in any language, loop logic, sleep timer, OTA-capable models on request.

Content

Pre-loaded video/audio (up to 8 GB), per-unit unique serial content for personalised campaigns.

Materials

FSC walnut/beech/bamboo, leather wraps, brass inlay, recycled PET, mother-of-pearl, optical acrylic.

Print & finish

CMYK + 5 spot colours, foil (gold/silver/copper/holographic), emboss, deboss, UV varnish, soft-touch lamination.

Packaging

Magnetic-close gift boxes, drawer boxes, sleeves, hangtags, ribbon ties, custom inserts and inserts.
