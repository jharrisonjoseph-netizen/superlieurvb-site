Acrylic Photo Frames

Museum-grade 30 mm optical acrylic. UV-printed photos that look like they're floating in glass — without a single scratch in sight.

Range overview

Crystal clarity, virtually unbreakable.

Each block is CNC-cut and polished from 30 mm optical-grade acrylic — 17× the impact resistance of glass and 50% lighter. Photos are direct UV-printed in 1440 dpi for archival-quality colour that won't fade for 20+ years.

  • 30 mm optical-grade cast acrylic (PMMA)
  • 1440 dpi UV LED direct print, archival inks
  • CNC bevelled edges & museum polish
  • Tabletop or wall-mount options
  • Lifetime warranty on material & print
  • Recyclable acrylic + FSC inner packaging
