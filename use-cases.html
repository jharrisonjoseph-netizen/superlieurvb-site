A closer look

Case study: 12,000-unit holiday campaign for a fintech.

Client. A US-based fintech serving 12,000 enterprise customers wanted to thank top accounts with something more memorable than wine.

Brief. 12,000 walnut video boxes preloaded with a unique 90-second message from the CEO, mailed door-to-door in November.

Delivery.

  • NDA + brief lock: Day 0
  • Custom box samples approved: Day 9
  • 12,000 units produced + serialised content: Day 32
  • DDP airfreight to LA 3PL: Day 38
  • USPS Priority drops to recipients: Day 44

Result. 71% of recipients posted unboxing videos on LinkedIn; the campaign generated 4.2× the pipeline of the prior year's wine programme at 60% of the budget.
